# HUMAN-INTERVENTION-MAP.md — Geolander

**Date:** 2026-08-14

**Why this document is unusually important here.** In a typical SEO engagement, most of the work is
automatable and a few items need a human. In this one, `[INFERENCE]` **the highest-value actions
identified are almost all human-dependent** — business decisions, relationships, photographs and service
quality. That is not a limitation of the analysis. It is what the evidence found: this market rewards
verifiable trust, and trust cannot be generated by a machine.

**The bluntest version:** an AI can write every page in this plan. It cannot decide whether you permit
customers to drive to Tusheti, it cannot photograph your cars, and it cannot earn you a review.

modelled. If long rentals turn out to be the profit engine, a whole cluster that appears nowhere in these
documents becomes first priority.

## Tier 4 — Things only the operator can produce

**impossible for an AI to fabricate honestly.**

| Asset | Why only Geolander |

## Tier 5 — The compounding operational habit

**This is the most important row in the document, and it is not a project.**

| Habit | Owner | Frequency |
|---|---|---|
| **Post-rental review request over WhatsApp** | Boris / staff | **Every single rental** |
| Respond to every review, in the reviewer's language | Boris | Within 48 h |
| Photo/video handover, sent to the customer on WhatsApp | Staff | Every rental |
| Ask *"how did you find us?"* in the first WhatsApp reply | Staff | Every enquiry |
| Log the booking outcome against its `GL-XXXX` reference | Boris | Every request |

`[INFERENCE]` No AI, agency or plugin can do any of these. Together they produce the review corpus, the
attribution data, and the dispute protection that the entire strategy depends on — and each costs about
two minutes.

**The honest framing:** if only one thing from this whole engagement gets done, it should be the review
request. It compounds, it is free, it cannot be copied, and `[OBSERVED]` it feeds the local pack, the
directory tier, the AI recommendation layer and the buyer's decision simultaneously.

## What AI genuinely can do here

To be fair to the other side of the ledger:

- All the research in this engagement — ~90 searches, ~40 competitor pages read, 64 customer-voice
  sources, and the adversarial review that refuted three of my own claims
- Draft every page, in every language
- The technical fixes — the redirect loop, the `$0` guard, schema, metadata, internal linking
- Monitoring, alerting and reporting
- Maintaining the calendar and permission table **once the facts are supplied**
- Preparing every outreach email

`[INFERENCE]` **The division is clean: AI supplies the analysis and the words. The human supplies the
facts, the decisions, the photographs, the relationships and the service.** In this particular market,
the second list is where nearly all the defensible value sits — which is unusual, and worth saying
plainly rather than obscuring.
