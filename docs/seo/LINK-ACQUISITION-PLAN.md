# LINK-ACQUISITION-PLAN.md — Geolander

**Date:** 2026-08-14 · Companion to `BACKLINK-GAP.md`

**Framing:** this is an **entity-presence plan**, not a link-count plan. The objective is that a
traveller researching Georgian car rental — and an AI system grounding an answer — encounters the name
"Geolander" from a source that is not Geolander. Whether a given placement passes PageRank is secondary.

---

## Tier 0 — Placements that require no outreach at all

`[INFERENCE]` **Do these first.** They are the highest yield per hour and involve no relationship risk.

### 0.1 · Google Business Profile → the directory tier, automatically

`[OBSERVED]` Wanderlog and wheree.com auto-generate business pages from Google Maps data. A fetched
Wanderlog page for a competitor showed Google attribution, "4.9 out of 5 from 2,707 reviews", verbatim
review quotes, photos, contact details and a map. **Neither site offers a submission or claim route.**

`[INFERENCE]` These citations cannot be earned by outreach. They are **caused** by a populated GBP with
genuine reviews. Getting the GBP right manufactures an entire tier of third-party pages that both
travellers and AI systems retrieve — with no email sent.

**Action:** `LOCAL-SEO-PLAN.md` Phases 0–3. **Effort: low. Value: highest in this document.**

### 0.2 · Trustpilot — claim the profile

`[OBSERVED]` Nine Georgian rental competitors hold Trustpilot profiles: werent.ge, tbilisocarrental.ge,
gsservices.ge, tbilisicars.com, travelcar.ge, cars4rent.ge, rentmotors.ge, cheapcarrental.ge,
rentautogeorgia.ge. **Geolander does not.** Localrent cites Trustpilot for its headline "4.8/5 from
4,509 reviews" `[OBSERVED]`.

`[INFERENCE]` Self-serve to create, and heavily weighted in AI retrieval as third-party trust evidence.
**One of the very few high-authority citation slots that can simply be claimed rather than earned.**

**Caveat `[OBSERVED]`:** the fetch of a Trustpilot competitor profile returned HTTP 403, so TrustScores
and review counts could not be verified. The *existence* of the profiles is from search-result titles.

**Action:** claim the profile, then feed it from the same post-rental WhatsApp flow as the GBP.
Alternate between GBP and Trustpilot requests so neither looks manufactured. **Effort: low.**

### 0.3 · TripAdvisor business listing

`[OBSERVED]` Competitors including RentCarPlus, RACE and Naniko hold TripAdvisor listings, and
TripAdvisor forum threads surfaced in more research queries than any other domain.

**Action:** create the business listing. **Do not post promotional replies in the forums** — `[OBSERVED]`
one was seen deleted mid-thread for violating self-promotional guidelines. The listing is the legitimate
route; the forum is not. **Effort: low.**

## Tier 1 — Direct editorial outreach (3 targets, high fit)

### 1.1 · ountravela.com — **the single best-fit target found**

`[OBSERVED]` A curated list of small 4×4 rental operators in Tbilisi and Kutaisi. Profiles five named
individuals — Temo, Joris, Nick, Martyna, Andreas & Svetlana — with per-vehicle price tables. Published
Feb 2022, **last modified July 2026** — actively maintained. Ranked on four separate 4×4 queries.

**Why they would want Geolander `[OBSERVED]`:**
- Stated selection basis: *"small, local, family-run businesses whose professionalism we've appreciated"*
- It runs a **lead-capture form per operator**: *"Your message will be sent to the rental company who
  will reply within 48 hours"* — a direct lead-gen relationship, which is **exactly the mechanic
  Geolander's WhatsApp-quote model already fits**
- Its listed operators run Foresters at €50–79 and Outlanders at €109–119, and 4Runners at €119–140.
  **Geolander's $26–120 range would be the most affordable entry on the page**
- `[OBSERVED]` It does not disclose how it earns revenue, so whether inclusion is editorial or paid is
  unknown — **ask directly rather than assume**

**The pitch — and note what it leads with:** not "please link to us", but the **permission answer**.
`[OBSERVED]` The decisive line in each of its existing entries is a permission statement — *"Driving in
Tusheti and Vashlovani is authorized"* / *"Driving prohibited at Tusheti"* / *"Tusheti authorized only
for Toyota 4runner and Tacoma models"*. Geolander's inclusion pitch is a clear, honest answer to that
same question, plus real per-car specs.

**Blocked on:** the route-permission decision. **Effort: low. Value: high.**

### 1.2 · geotravelmarket.com — **the most explicitly open route**

`[OBSERVED]` Its page *"Best 4x4 and Hybrid Car Rental in Georgia | Mountain Travel Guide"* ranked on
two independent queries and **currently features only two operators** (Rentigo, Carex Rent Car) — a
thinly populated category.

`[OBSERVED]` It carries a direct solicitation: *"If you'd like your company featured in our blog, get in
touch — or list your services directly and start receiving bookings"*, with a `/become-vendor` route.

`[OBSERVED]` It claims vetting — *"vetting local hosts"*, *"trusted vendors"*, *"verified providers"* —
but the approval standards are not published.

`[INFERENCE]` A thinly-populated, on-topic category that is actively asking for vendors is the
lowest-friction placement available. **Effort: very low. Value: medium-high.**

### 1.3 · wander-lush.org — **the highest-value, slowest, and most easily botched**

`[OBSERVED]` The strongest editorial property in the niche: appeared in 5 of 21 searches including the
head term, ~15,000-word driving guide, 105 reader comments, dated 2026 and actively maintained. The
author is simultaneously the resident expert on the TripAdvisor Georgia forum with 440+ posts. She names
only three non-chain entities: **Local Rent, GoTrip, and Martyna z Gruzji**.

`[OBSERVED]` Explicit affiliate disclosure — *"This post contains affiliate links, meaning I may earn a
commission"* — so placement is commercially negotiable.

**`[INFERENCE]` But do not lead with money.** Her recommendation is currently *captured by an
aggregator*, justified on *"better prices, more flexible terms, and vehicles that are better suited to
Georgian roads"*. The route in is **editorial usefulness**, not a commission offer:

Her guide addresses restricted roads and states that driving them *"can void your insurance"*, and that
*"many companies use GPS trackers"* — **but there is no reference table.** A maintained, honest
**road-permission table and road-opening calendar** is something she would plausibly cite because it
makes her own guide better. That is a legitimate reason to link, which is the only kind that works on
someone who has been pitched a thousand times.

**Sequence:** build the asset first. Approach second. **Effort: medium. Timeline: months, not weeks.**

## Tier 2 — Distribution channels (bookings, not citations)

### 2.1 · Localrent

`[OBSERVED]` The most-cited third-party property in the editorial layer — the named recommendation in at
least five independent sources. ~1,133 cars from local Georgian suppliers on its Tbilisi page.

**But `[OBSERVED]`: it does not name its suppliers.** Its page says only *"We partner with trusted car
rental companies in Tbilisi"*.

`[INFERENCE]` **A Localrent listing buys bookings and price-comparison presence, and essentially zero
brand-entity reinforcement. An AI citing Localrent will never learn the name "Geolander" from it.**
Budget it as a **sales channel**, not as SEO.

**Two hazards `[OBSERVED]`:** its Tbilisi page lists an Outlander at **$19.00/day** against Geolander's
$26–28 floor for the same model — so listing there anchors against your own direct price. And
`[OBSERVED]` its terms restrict renters to **paved roads only**, which sits in direct tension with a
permission-led positioning. Listing on Localrent while marketing permission to drive Tusheti is a
contradiction customers will notice.

**Recommendation `[INFERENCE]`:** a **considered yes, with the tension named** — take the volume for the
standard rentals, keep the mountain-permission product direct-only, and make sure the site explains why
booking direct differs. Do not treat this as a link-building action.

### 2.2 · Russian affiliate listicles (vc.ru, dtf.ru, tip-to-trip, georgia.in-facts)

`[OBSERVED]` vc.ru returned on five separate Russian queries with five different URLs. These are
monetised — cloaked affiliate redirects (`swiy.co/localrent-vc-tbilisi`, `swiy.co/discovercars-v`).
`[OBSERVED]` Their TOP-6 lists contain **six aggregators and not one Georgian operator**.

`[INFERENCE]` The pitch writes itself: *"your list of the best car rental in Georgia contains no actual
Georgian company."* Commercially negotiable, but treat as paid placement and evaluate on booking ROI,
not on link value.

**Defer until:** the `/ru/` redirect loop is fixed and native Russian content exists. `[INFERENCE]`
Sending Russian traffic to a broken Russian homepage would waste the placement.

## Tier 3 — Local partnerships (slow, compounding, human-only)

`[INFERENCE]` These cannot be automated and are listed in `HUMAN-INTERVENTION-MAP.md`.

| Target | Genuine mutual interest |
|---|---|
| Tbilisi guesthouses, boutique hotels, hostels | Their guests need cars; free hotel delivery is already offered. Reciprocal referral, not a favour |
| Kazbegi / Gudauri / Mestia accommodation | `[INFERENCE]` Guests arriving without a car are a booking constraint for *them* |
| Hiking and trekking tour operators | Trailheads with no public transport are the strongest self-drive use case |
| Georgian wine tourism (Kakheti) | `[CUSTOMER]` But note: for wine-tasting days a **driver** is the honest recommendation. Partner accordingly rather than overreaching |
| Caucasus travel Facebook groups and expat communities | Answer questions genuinely; do not post offers |
| Georgian National Tourism Administration and regional tourism bodies | `[INFERENCE]` A maintained road-opening calendar is a public-good asset a tourism body might reference |

## The asset-first principle

`[INFERENCE]` Three of the four highest-value placements above are unlocked by the **same two assets**,
not by outreach volume:

1. **The route-permission table** — `CONTENT-GAP.md` CREATE-1. Unlocks ountravela (permission is their
   decisive field), gives wander-lush a reason to cite, and answers the highest-intent query in the market
2. **The road-opening calendar** — `CONTENT-GAP.md` CREATE-7. The strongest linkable-asset candidate
   identified: seasonal, referenced repeatedly, cheap to maintain, and genuinely useful to competitors'
   own audiences

**Build the assets, then do the outreach.** Outreach without them is a request for a favour; outreach
with them is an offer.

## Explicitly not doing

Paid link networks · PBNs · guest-post farms · mass directory submission (`[OBSERVED]` the directories
that matter here cannot be submitted to) · TripAdvisor or Reddit astroturfing (`[OBSERVED]` deleted,
and visible) · fabricated reviews anywhere (risks the GBP, the most valuable asset identified) ·
optimising for referring-domain count as a KPI.

**Measure instead:** relevant third-party properties on which "Geolander" appears alongside a
disambiguating token (Tbilisi / Caucasus / Sakartvelo), and bookings attributable to each.
