# PAGE-LEVEL-SEO-BLUEPRINT.md — Geolander

**Date:** 2026-08-14

**Scope:** the eight strategically decisive pages. `[INFERENCE]` Specifying all ~70 routes would produce
a document nobody reads and would imply a precision the evidence does not support. These eight carry the
Golden Path; the rest follow their patterns.

**A rule that applies to every page below:** *answer first, sell second.* `[OBSERVED]` The most common
competitor failure in this market is intent mismatch — rentcarsgeorgia.com's mountain page carries ~3,000
words of destination detail under an H1 that is a pure CTA (*"Book Your Mountain SUV"*), and starcar.ge's
top-ranking guides carry **no prices at all**, forcing the comparison shopper to leave. Almost nobody
executes this cleanly, which is the opening.

**No word counts are prescribed.** `[INFERENCE]` Competitors rank at 600 words and at 3,000. Length is
an output of answering the question completely, not an input.

| **Title** | `{Year} {Make} {Model} — Rent in Tbilisi from ${price}/day \| Geolander` |

## Applies to every page

- **Do not pad.** `[OBSERVED]` Competitors rank at 600 words. Answer the question and stop
- **Publish prices.** `[OBSERVED]` starcar.ge's top-ranking guides have none, and that is a gift
- **Date everything factual.** `[OBSERVED]` TripAdvisor's best-ranking threads are 7–10 years old and
  serve a decade-old price to 2026 searchers. Currency is a competitive advantage here
- **One intent per URL.** `[OBSERVED]` rentcarsgeorgia.com carries Svaneti, Tusheti and Kazbegi on one
  page, so none gets a dedicated URL
- **Every page needs a conversion path**, even informational ones — but the CTA should match the stage
- **No `FAQPage` expansion.** `[FIRST-PARTY]` FAQ rich results ended 7 May 2026. Keep the visible FAQ;
  stop investing in the markup
- **Disambiguate.** `[OBSERVED]` The unqualified word "Georgia" retrieves the US state in 4 of 10
  results. Use Tbilisi, Caucasus, Sakartvelo, "Georgia (country)"
