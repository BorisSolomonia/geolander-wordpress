**A caveat that should govern how this document is read `[INFERENCE]`:** a 12-month SEO roadmap written

| Objective | Why this and not a traffic number |

| **Recognised as the operator that answers the permission question honestly** | The only differentiator identified that competitors structurally cannot copy |
| **Compounding review velocity across GBP and Trustpilot** | `[INFERENCE]` The most durable asset available, and entirely under Geolander's control |

**Deliberately absent: any traffic, ranking or backlink target.** `[INFERENCE]` With fifteen cars and
hard-capped capacity, traffic past peak utilisation is worth nothing, and setting a number would push
the programme toward volume the business cannot serve.

## Seasonality

`[OBSERVED]` The Abano Pass is open roughly early June to early October. Winter tyres are mandatory
Dec 1 – Mar 1. Batumi demand is sharply summer-weighted.

`[INFERENCE]` Content must be published **ahead** of its season, not during it — indexing and ranking
take time this market does not give back:

| Publish by | For |
|---|---|
| **March** | Mountain, permission and route content, ahead of the June–October season |
| **September** | Winter driving, ski, snow-chain and winter-tyre content |
| **January** | Spring planning content — road openings, Kakheti, wine season |

`[INFERENCE]` **The road-opening calendar must be updated in April or early May every year**, before the
question is asked. A stale calendar is worse than none, because the whole value of the asset is that it
is current.

## What would make this roadmap wrong

Listed so it can be corrected rather than defended:

1. **GSC shows the site was never indexed** → months 1–3 change entirely; indexation becomes the whole job
2. **The local pack is unwinnable from postcode 0108** → Q1's centrepiece loses its ranking payoff, though
   reviews and entity work stay load-bearing
3. **The permission answer is "no"** → the flagship is deleted, and Q2 pulls forward
4. **Long rentals turn out to be the profit engine** → an entire cluster (monthly, relocation, long-stay)
   that appears nowhere in this document becomes first priority. `[INFERENCE]` **The most likely single
   thing this plan is missing**
5. **A locale-correct SERP shows something completely different** → `[OBSERVED]` nobody in this engagement
   has seen a real google.ge result. This is not a remote possibility
6. **Bookings come overwhelmingly from a channel nobody measured** — repeat customers, hotel referrals,
   walk-ins → the ROI case for organic changes shape entirely

`[INFERENCE]` **Any one of these should trigger a rewrite rather than an adjustment.** A roadmap this
far ahead of its evidence earns its keep by being falsifiable, not by being followed.
