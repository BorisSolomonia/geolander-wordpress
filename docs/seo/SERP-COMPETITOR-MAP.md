# SERP-COMPETITOR-MAP.md — who actually beats Geolander in search

**Date:** 2026-08-14 · **Method:** ~45 independent searches across English, Russian, Georgian,
Ukrainian, French, Arabic and Chinese, run by three parallel research agents, with **~25 ranking pages
fetched and read in full** rather than judged from titles.

---

## Method limits — read before trusting any statement here

Stated first, because every conclusion below is conditioned by them.

1. **The search tool is US-locale.** It returns organic-style link lists only. **No local pack, no AI
   Overviews, no ads, no numeric rank positions were visible on any query.** Consequently **not one
   rank position is stated anywhere in this document.** Phrases like "top result" mean "first in the
   list this tool returned", never "ranks #1 in Google".
2. **A searcher in Tbilisi, Moscow or Kyiv sees materially different results** — more geo-proximate
   `.ge` domains, and a local pack that is invisible here.
3. **Yandex could not be queried at all.** `[INFERENCE]` A large share of the Russian-language audience
   researches on Yandex. Any Russian-language plan built only on this data is built on the minority
   engine for that audience.
4. **Mitigation:** agents fetched and read the actual competitor pages rather than inferring from
   titles. Where a page could not be loaded, that is stated rather than guessed.
5. **Failed fetches, named honestly:** `forum.awd.ru` (404 — so **no verbatim Russian forum language**
   about the Omalo road or contract restrictions), `otzovik.com` MyRentACar reviews (timeout — **no
   Russian complaint data**), `zhuanlan.zhihu.com` (robots-disallowed — **no verbatim Chinese customer
   language**), `civil.ge` (robots-disallowed), `rentacar-tbilisi.ru` (403).

**No search volume, keyword difficulty, domain authority, traffic estimate or referring-domain count
appears in this document, because no tool capable of measuring them was available. Any such figure
would have been invented.**

## Finding 0 — Geolander does not rank. Anywhere. And the brand name is part of why

`[OBSERVED]` Four independent visibility probes returned **zero** results for geo-lander.com:

| Probe | Returned |
|---|---|
| `Geolander car rental Tbilisi` | georentcar.ge, geocarrental.ge, rentalcars.com, localrent.com, Europcar, Enterprise, geodrive.info, Expedia. **No geo-lander.com** |
| `"geo-lander.com"` (quoted, exact domain) | Wikipedia *"Geo (landform)"*, *"Lander"*, *"LandSerf"*, and an unrelated South African `geolander.co.za`. **No geo-lander.com** |
| `"Geolander" + Subaru Forester + Outlander` | An Instagram page titled *"Geolander Tires On Subaru Forester 17 Inch Wheels"*; a Subaru group post about fitting *"Yokohama Geolander"* tyres |
| `"Geolander" OR "Geo-Lander" car rental Georgia 4x4` | Expedia and Kayak pages for the **US state** of Georgia |

**Competitors outrank Geolander on Geolander's own brand name** `[OBSERVED]` — georentcar.ge and
geocarrental.ge, almost certainly on "Geo" + rental entity similarity.

**The structural cause `[OBSERVED]`:** "Geolander" is a near-homograph of **Yokohama GEOLANDAR**, a
global all-terrain SUV tyre line — occupying the *same semantic space* (SUV, 4×4, all-terrain) and
bound to the *same vehicle models* Geolander stocks. `[INFERENCE]` No volume of content wins a
bare-brand query against a global tyre manufacturer. The response is entity disambiguation (GBP,
consistent NAP, third-party corroboration) plus targeting **compound phrases** — "Geolander car
rental", "Geolander Tbilisi" — never the bare name. Developed in `LOCAL-SEO-PLAN.md` Part 2.

## Finding 1 — The English market is three separate SERPs, not one

This is the most important structural finding in the research, and it dictates where to compete.

### Layer A — Head terms: **locked. Do not compete.**

`[OBSERVED]` Across `car rental tbilisi`, `rent a car tbilisi`, `tbilisi airport car rental`,
`car hire georgia country`, `rent a car batumi`, `car rental kutaisi airport`, the returned set was
almost entirely international brands and OTAs: **Sixt** (on six queries), Europcar, Enterprise, Alamo,
Avis, Hertz, Green Motion, Expedia, Skyscanner, Kayak, Booking.com, Trip.com, Rentalcars.com,
Rentcars.com, Carflexi.

**Exactly one Georgian operator** broke into a head-term SERP: `rentcarsgeorgia.com` on
"tbilisi airport car rental" `[OBSERVED]`.

`[INFERENCE]` These queries are unwinnable for a near-zero-authority site and should not be primary
targets. Their only strategic use is as a **price anchor**: observed OTA hooks were Skyscanner
"from $34", Kayak "from £20/day" and "Tbilisi car rentals from $22/day", Expedia "$34" `[OBSERVED]`.
Geolander's $26–28 entry point is credible against that anchor.

### Layer B — The "no deposit" layer: a commodity knife-fight

`[OBSERVED]` `cheap car rental tbilisi no deposit` and `car rental georgia no deposit` returned an
almost entirely different set, with **near-zero OTA presence**: roscar.ge, carwaytbilisi.com,
rentalcartbilisi.com, gcartbilisi.com, gcargeorgia.com, tbilisicar.com, cheapcarrentaltbilisi.com,
geocarrent.ge, 4drivegeorgia.com, rentcarfy.com, roscartbilisi.com.

The literal string **"No Deposit, No Credit Card"** was observed in the title tags of roscar.ge,
carwaytbilisi.com, gcartbilisi.com, tbilisicar.com, gcargeorgia.com, 4drivegeorgia.com and
rentcarfy.com `[OBSERVED]`.

`[INFERENCE]` A dozen interchangeable operators, exact-match domains, title tags restating the query.
Two agents fetched representatives:

- **carwaytbilisi.com** — top of the list, and **four vehicles**, no reviews, no testimonials, no date
  picker, 4×4 barely mentioned `[OBSERVED]`. Prices: Prius $30, Elantra $35, RAV4 $45, Outlander $50
- **roscar.ge** — genuinely broad (traffic rules, parking, tolls, destination pages, 18-question FAQ)
  but **only three testimonials** against a self-reported 9.6/10 with no third-party link, and its
  "no deposit" promise is undercut by a required 15–20% prepayment `[OBSERVED]`. SUVs from €29/day

**Verdict `[INFERENCE]`:** winnable on substance, but it is a **price fight**. roscar.ge's €29 SUV entry
and Localrent's $19 SUV tile sit at or below Geolander's floor. Enter this layer with the honest
inclusions story, not with a price claim. "No deposit" alone is not a differentiator when eleven
competitors have it in their title tag.

### Layer C — The 4×4 / mountain layer: **soft, fragmented, and Geolander's natural home**

`[OBSERVED]` Adding "4x4" strips out most OTA dominance. `4x4 car rental tbilisi` returned
4x4carrental.ge, tbilisocarrental.ge, fstarentcar.com, a Facebook page and Overlando — small
independents and one exact-match domain, with only Sixt and Kayak representing the big players.

**How weak the incumbents actually are, from pages that were fetched and read:**

| Competitor | Ranking page | Observed weakness |
|---|---|---|
| **4x4carrental.ge** | `/` on the core 4×4 term | **Unreplaced `Lorem ipsum` placeholder text still on the page.** No prices anywhere. No booking mechanism, no date picker, no FAQ, no reviews, no blog, no destination content. Contact is a **Gmail address**. Fleet is older manual Hilux/Land Cruiser stock with two apparent duplicate listings `[OBSERVED]` |
| **rentcarsgeorgia.com** | `/suv-rental-georgia-mountains/` | ~2,800–3,200 words of genuine destination depth — **and no H1 at all**, running on H2s only. For Tusheti it suggests hiring someone else, forfeiting the highest-intent booking. One page carries Svaneti, Tusheti and Kazbegi, so none gets a dedicated URL `[OBSERVED]` |
| **starcar.ge** | two blog posts, both on one SERP | ~2,500–3,200 words, 25+ named Georgian locations, ground-clearance specs — **but no prices anywhere in the guide**, and the FAQ is plain Q&A with no schema. Content sits on `/blog/` and does not feed the money pages `[OBSERVED]` |
| **tbilisocarrental.ge** | `/4x4-car-rental-in-georgia/` | ~1,100 words of generic terrain talk — **no named destinations at all**: no Tusheti, Svaneti, Kazbegi, Abano or Juta. No reviews, no FAQ on-page, no date picker `[OBSERVED]` |
| **fstarentcar.com** | `/4x4-car-rental-tbilisi-georgia/` | The most complete execution found — 15 vehicles with visible rates, date form, 8 testimonials, FAQ, travel-guide link cluster. **But Svaneti and Abano Pass are absent from the money page** (depth lives in the blog), no live pricing, minimal technical specs, and it spreads across Georgia, Armenia, Turkey and Azerbaijan, diluting topical focus `[OBSERVED]` |

`[INFERENCE]` **A site with unreplaced lorem ipsum and zero pricing ranks on the primary commercial
4×4 keyword.** That is the clearest possible evidence that this layer is not defended by quality.

## Finding 2 — The single biggest opportunity: the roads everyone else forbids

This emerged independently across two agents and is the most commercially loaded finding in the
research.

**`[OBSERVED]` Localrent — the dominant aggregator, and the operator wander-lush.org recommends first —
states flatly on its own page:**

> *"If you rent a car on Localrent, you can drive only on paved roads."*

It names **three strictly forbidden routes** — Akhaltsikhe–Batumi via Goderdzi Pass,
Mestia–Ushguli–Lentekhi, Sairme–Abastumani — and **seven strictly forbidden regions** — Truso, Juta,
Vashlovani, Tusheti and Omalo, Shatili, Abkhazia, South Ossetia. It adds: *"Insurance does not cover
these routes, and any damage or accident is the responsibility of the renter."*

**And it is not alone `[OBSERVED]`:**

- **carrentdeme.com** ranks well on Kazbegi terms with ~3,000 words of genuinely local detail — while
  **explicitly forbidding Tusheti, Vashlovani and Juta** to its own vehicles
- **rentcarsgeorgia.com** tells Tusheti searchers to hire a driver instead
- `[INFERENCE]` Sixt and the international brands will certainly prohibit unpaved mountain routes

**`[OBSERVED]` wander-lush.org — the highest-authority independent voice in this niche — reports that
4WD is *contractually mandatory* for Tusheti, Shatili and Vashlovani.**

So the market has a hard, verifiable, high-intent gap: **travellers are told they need a 4×4 for these
places, and then told by nearly every rental company that they may not drive there.**

`[INFERENCE]` **If — and only if — Geolander's own policy genuinely permits these routes, that is the
strongest differentiator available**, and it is not copyable by an aggregator (whose policy is set by
its insurers) or by an international brand.

**This is a business question, not an SEO one, and it must be answered before it is marketed.**
`[OBSERVED]` Geolander's own `/terms/` §8 currently prohibits *"off-road driving (unless vehicle is
specifically approved)"* — which, read literally, puts Geolander in the **same** position as the
competitors it could otherwise beat. **Resolving that clause is the highest-leverage decision in this
entire engagement.** See `GOLDEN-PATH.md`.

## Finding 3 — The strongest counter-evidence, stated plainly

The brief demands that conflicts be shown rather than smoothed over. This one cuts against the 4×4
strategy and must not be buried.

**The most credible voices in this niche are actively arguing that you do not need a 4×4 in Georgia.**

`[OBSERVED]` wander-lush.org: *"For most routes in Georgia, a standard sedan is perfectly adequate."*
On Tusheti: *"The road to Tusheti (the Abano Pass) has been transformed in the past two years, and is
now wider, smoother and faster than before."* On Svaneti: *"Mestia to Ushguli – fully concreted as of
2024."*

`[OBSERVED]` Russian publishers say the same, harder. georgia.in-facts.info: *"Почти везде нормальный
асфальт, можно проехать даже на обычной пузотерке"* (asphalt is fine almost everywhere, even a low-slung
car will do) and *"Летом нет необходимости во внедорожнике"* (in summer there is no need for an SUV).
tip-to-trip.com adds an economic argument: old 4×4s exceed 20 L/100 km in the mountains.

`[CUSTOMER]` And travellers themselves, on TripAdvisor: *"You do not need a 4x4 car, but need a car
with a good clearance which in most of the cases is SUV."* / *"You can use a normal car, like sedan or
minivan."* The thread's original poster settled on a Hyundai Tucson.

**`[OBSERVED]` Worse, a competitor is already publishing the technical argument against Geolander's own
fleet.** starcar.ge: *"The Subaru Forester uses symmetrical AWD — a permanent system … excellent for
slippery tarmac, mud, and light off-road but **has no low-range gearbox for extreme terrain**."*
Seven of Geolander's 15 vehicles are Foresters.

**`[INFERENCE]` What this means — and it is a genuine strategic constraint, not a footnote:**

1. **Do not build messaging on "you need a 4×4".** The most authoritative sources in the market are
   documenting the opposite, the roads are objectively improving, and a claim that contradicts
   wander-lush will lose.
2. **The defensible claims are narrower and true:** *ground clearance*, *winter*, *specific named
   routes*, and *permission to drive them*. Even the sceptics concede these — in-facts.info admits
   *"Зимой… вариант с внедорожником приобретает смысл"* (in winter the SUV option starts to make sense),
   and tip-to-trip's fuel objection targets old body-on-frame jeeps, **not a Forester or Crosstrek**.
3. **Publishing real per-model fuel consumption and ground clearance answers both objections using the
   objectors' own logic** — and it is data only the fleet owner has.
4. `[CUSTOMER]` Real travellers converge on **clearance, winter tyres and driver experience** — not on
   "4×4". Match the customer's vocabulary, not the marketing department's.

## Finding 4 — Russian is a separate market — ⚠ **PARTLY SUPERSEDED**

> **The claim that Russian is a *bigger opportunity* was refuted at high confidence** — see `STRATEGY-STRESS-TEST.md` Refutation 3. Russian is the **most densely served** layer, not an under-served one. What survives: the RU SERPs are structurally more *open* (no global OTAs). **Do not fund a Russian content programme.**

`[OBSERVED]` The English and Russian competitive sets overlap by roughly **25%**. Only cars4rent.ge and
rentalauto.ge appeared on both the English and Russian 4×4 SERPs.

| Language | SERP character `[OBSERVED]` |
|---|---|
| **Russian** | Top of the SERP is **not rental companies** — it is Russian UGC affiliate listicles. vc.ru returned on **five separate queries** with five different URLs ("ТОП-6 лучших сервисов проката без кредитной карты", "Аренда авто в Грузии 2026: рейтинг сервисов"), plus dtf.ru, in-trips.ru, tip-to-trip.com, georgia.in-facts.info. These are monetised — vc.ru links out through cloaked redirects (`swiy.co/localrent-vc-tbilisi`, `swiy.co/discovercars-v`). **But underneath them sits a deep long tail of local `.ge` operators ranking directly** — `аренда внедорожника грузия 4х4` returned **eight local operators** with zero OTAs above them |
| **Georgian (ka)** | **Not a rental-brand SERP at all.** Both queries returned only classifieds and directories: manqanebi.ge (5 URLs), servisebi.ge (3 URLs), gancxadebebi.ge. Peer-to-peer listings. **No rental company homepage ranked** |
| **Ukrainian** | Collapses into Russian — four of five results were Russian-language pages |
| **French** | Total OTA/brand wall: petitfute, carigami, hertz.fr, europcar, sixt, localrent, oneclickdrive. **Zero Georgian operators** |
| **Arabic** | The hardest wall found: oneclickdrive, rentalcars, booking.com/cars, kayak, vipcars, carngo, skyscanner.com.sa, europcar. **Zero `.ge` domains** |
| **Chinese** | Walled-garden UGC, not search-addressable: zuzuche.com (4 URLs of user rental reviews), zhihu, tuniu, autohome, youxiake |

**Audience evidence `[OBSERVED]`, from named sources:** Interfax (16 Jan 2026), citing the Georgian
Economy Ministry's National Tourism Administration — **Russia was the largest single source of
travellers to Georgia in 2025 with 1.61 million arrivals, up 13.4% year on year.** GBC.ge for Q4 2025:
*"the highest number of visitors came from Russia (21.8% of total arrivals), followed by Turkey (18.6%)
and Armenia (14.9%)."*

The agent flagged an honest complication: different sources gave different 2025 totals (7.8M vs 6.1M
vs "nearly 7 million") — almost certainly different metrics (arrivals vs visits vs visitors), and
`civil.ge` was robots-blocked so it could not be reconciled. **The ranking (Russia #1) and the share
(21.8%) are consistent across sources; the absolute total is not, and is therefore not relied upon.**

`[INFERENCE]` There is no comparable single English-speaking bloc — UK/US/Australia/Canada arrivals
appear in no top-three breakdown. The English-language audience is real but fragmented across many
small national markets. **The Russian-reading audience is one large bloc, and Armenian visitors (#3)
are substantially Russian-reading too.**

**The bar for entry, and it is high `[OBSERVED]`:** the Russian incumbents are writing *native* Russian,
not machine translation. carrental-georgia.com's ~8,000-word Russian jeep guide uses colloquialisms no
translation engine produces — *"паркетник"* for a crossover, *"гелик"* for the G-Class. ex-cars.com is
**Russian-first**, with English demoted to an "Eng" link — the exact inverse of Geolander's
English-default architecture. Operators are buying `.ru` domains for this segment.

`[INFERENCE]` **A machine-translated `/ru/` locale will not compete.** And Geolander's `/ru/` homepage
is currently in a redirect loop (see `TECHNICAL-SEO-AUDIT.md` P0-0), while `/ru/fleet/` serves an
English title and English body `[OBSERVED]`.

**The Russian modifier stack worth owning `[OBSERVED]`:** «без залога» / «без депозита» (no deposit),
«без кредитной карты» (no credit card), «карты МИР» (MIR cards), «для россиян», «отзывы» (reviews),
«внедорожник» (SUV), and year stamps («2026»). `[INFERENCE]` Every one of these is an operator-specific
policy question that **no aggregator can answer as credibly as a named operator**.

## Where to compete — the direct answer

| Compete | Do not compete |
|---|---|
| 4×4 / mountain / route-specific intent (English **and** Russian) | Head terms: "car rental tbilisi", "rent a car georgia" |
| The **permitted-routes** gap — if policy allows it | Price. Localrent SUV from $19; Kayak from $22 |
| ~~Russian-language commercial intent~~ — **refuted, see above** | Russian content programme · Arabic, Chinese, French — total OTA walls, zero local penetration |
| Named-destination guides: Kazbegi, Svaneti/Mestia, Tusheti/Omalo, Juta, Truso, Goderdzi | Town-name terms like "rent a car Mestia" — Expedia-family programmatic pages saturate them and the SERP is, as one agent put it, a wasteland |
| Local pack via GBP — plausibly the biggest near-term lever | Georgian-language rental terms — classifieds own that SERP |
| Placement on wander-lush, ountravela, TripAdvisor, vc.ru-class listicles | Bare-brand "Geolander" — Yokohama owns it |
| Ground clearance, winter, real fuel consumption, per-model specs | "You need a 4×4" as a claim — the authorities contradict it |
